Anthony Edwards

"Ant-Man" — Minnesota Timberwolves

About Anthony

Anthony Edwards was born on August 5, 2001, in Atlanta, Georgia. Selected first overall by the Minnesota Timberwolves in the 2020 NBA Draft after one standout season at the University of Georgia, he quickly became one of the league's most explosive young scorers, known for his athleticism, highlight dunks, and clutch shooting.

Career Timeline

  1. 2019 — Named a McDonald's All-American in high school
  2. 2019–2020 — Played one season at the University of Georgia, named SEC Freshman of the Year
  3. 2020 — Drafted 1st overall by the Minnesota Timberwolves
  4. 2021 — Named to the NBA All-Rookie First Team
  5. 2023 — Selected to his first NBA All-Star Game
  6. 2024–2025 — Named to the All-NBA Second Team in back-to-back seasons

Key Achievements

  • 1st overall pick, 2020 NBA Draft
  • Multiple-time NBA All-Star
  • NBA All-Star Game MVP
  • Two-time All-NBA Second Team selection
  • Known as one of the most electrifying dunkers in the league

Edwards has grown from a promising rookie into a franchise cornerstone, leading Minnesota through deep playoff runs and establishing himself as one of the faces of the modern NBA.
